Dawn Brew

Hand-roasted coffee, crafted with care in Karnal.

Our Origin

Every bean is sourced from small farms committed to sustainable growing practices. We believe quality begins at the root — healthy soil, ethical labor, and time-honored cultivation.

The Craft of Brewing

Our roasting process is entirely hands-on. Each batch is monitored for aroma, color, and temperature to unlock the full character of the bean. This isn’t mass production — it’s a ritual.

Coming to Karnal

Summer 2026 marks the opening of our first café in the heart of Karnal. Designed for slow mornings and deep conversations, it will be a space where community and craftsmanship meet.
