Our Origin

Born from early mornings and slow roasts, Dawn Brew began as a quiet ritual in a Karnal kitchen. What started with a single drum roaster has grown into a passion for beans that tell a story — from farm to first light.

The Art of Brewing

We believe coffee should be felt, not just tasted. Each batch is hand-roasted in small quantities, allowing us to honor the bean’s origin, season, and soul. No automation. No shortcuts. Just time, heat, and attention.

Find Us in Karnal

Nestled near the old market square, our space is designed for stillness. Wooden counters, warm light, and the quiet hum of grinding beans. Come for the coffee. Stay for the calm.
