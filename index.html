Dawn Brew

Hand-roasted in Karnal. Opening Summer 2026.

Our Origin

Born from quiet mornings and the rich soil of Karnal, Dawn Brew began as a small dream to craft coffee with intention. Every bean is sourced from ethical farms, roasted in small batches, and handled with care.

The Art of Brewing

We believe coffee is more than a drink — it's a ritual. Our roasting process honors tradition while embracing precision, bringing out nuanced flavors in every cup. From first light to final pour, we do it by hand.

Coming to Karnal

Nestled in the heart of Karnal, our upcoming café will be a haven for early risers and slow sippers alike. Warm wood, soft light, and the scent of fresh roast — a space designed to welcome the day.
